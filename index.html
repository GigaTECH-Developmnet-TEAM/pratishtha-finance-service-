Who We Are

Building Financial Confidence

Pratishtha Finance Services – Pratishtha Finance Services is an MSME-Registered Financial Services Company dedicated to helping individuals, professionals, and small business owners access financial solutions with a simple and transparent process. Founded by Karan Gautam (Founder) and Sarita Kumari (Co-Founder), our vision is to make financial services more accessible by connecting customers with a broad network of 116+ Banks & NBFCs. Whether you need a Business Loan, Personal Loan, Home Loan, Loan Against Property (LAP), or MSME Loan, our experienced team assists you throughout the application process—from eligibility assessment and documentation to lender coordination.
