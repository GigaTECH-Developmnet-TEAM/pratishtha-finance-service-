FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pratishtha Finance Services?▼
Pratishtha Finance Services is an MSME Registered loan services firm that partners with 116+ banks and NBFCs to get you a loan at the best rate.
What is the PFS EMI Card?▼
The PFS EMI Card is our virtual PFS Shopping Card for quick everyday needs: an emergency loan up to ₹5,000, shopping on EMI, loan payments and more. It is not a credit card. Limits, charges and approval are subject to eligibility and lending-partner policy.
Is the PFS Shopping Card a credit card?▼
No. The PFS Shopping Card is a virtual card and is not a credit card. Card limit, charges and approval depend on your eligibility and the lending partner's policy, and are explained before you proceed.
How does a Gold Loan work?▼
You pledge gold ornaments or coins and receive up to 75% of the gold value as a loan, from ₹10,000 to ₹1 Crore, with rates from 9.00% p.a. You need Aadhaar, PAN and the gold items. Use the Gold Loan Estimator on our website for an indicative amount.
Which areas do you serve?▼
We serve customers all over India with a special focus on Purvanchal – Gorakhpur, Maharajganj, Kushinagar, Deoria, Basti, Azamgarh, Varanasi, Ballia, Ghazipur, Jaunpur and nearby districts. You can apply online or on WhatsApp from anywhere.
How much loan can I get?▼
You can get a loan from ₹10,000 up to ₹5 Crore, depending on the loan type.
What is the interest rate?▼
Rates start from 7.35% p.a. MSME loans get the lowest rates. The actual rate depends on your eligibility.
How long does loan approval take?▼
Approval typically takes 24-48 hours in normal cases. Emergency loans can even be approved the same day.
Is there any processing fee?▼
Pratishtha Finance Services does not charge any fee. Bank/NBFC processing fees may apply.
What CIBIL score do I need?▼
A CIBIL score of 750+ is ideal. However, we can still find options with some banks for scores of 650+.
What documents are needed for a personal loan?▼
Aadhaar, PAN, salary slip/income proof, 3 months' bank statement, and a photo.
What is the eligibility for a business loan?▼
The business should be 2+ years old, GST registered, have filed ITR, and have 12 months of bank statements.
What is an MSME loan?▼
An MSME loan is specially designed for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises – with rates starting from 7.35%.
What is a Udyam Certificate and why is it needed?▼
A Udyam Certificate is proof of MSME registration – it gets you a loan at a concessional rate.
What is the age limit for a home loan?▼
18 to 70 years. The tenure should be such that the loan closes before you turn 75.
Can I get a loan against property?▼
Yes, a Loan Against Property (LAP) lets you mortgage residential or commercial property to get a loan – up to 70% of the property value.
How long does a vehicle loan take?▼
Disbursal happens within 2-3 days once documentation is complete.
How much money can I get on a gold loan?▼
You can get up to 75% of the current market value of your gold – rates start from 9%.
Is a co-applicant required for an education loan?▼
Yes, a parent/guardian must be a co-applicant along with the student.
What is a working capital loan?▼
It's a short-term loan for a business's day-to-day operations – such as purchasing stock or paying salaries.
Is prepayment possible?▼
Yes, prepayment is possible on most loans. Some banks charge a penalty – please confirm beforehand.
What is a group loan?▼
A group of 5+ people can take a loan together – it's a great option for women's SHG groups.
How do I become an associate?▼
Message us on WhatsApp: +91 7233023547. There are three options – Loan Associate, Referral, and Branch Partner.
How much commission does a loan associate get?▼
Commission depends on the loan amount and type. Contact us for details.
Is doorstep service available?▼
Yes, our executive will visit your home to complete the documentation.
How do I apply online?▼
Click the Apply Now button on this website or message us on WhatsApp: +91 7233023547.
How do I track my application?▼
You can check your status in the Track Application section using your Application ID and mobile number.
What is the privacy policy?▼
Your data stays 100% secure – it is never shared with third parties. Click the Privacy Policy button for details.
